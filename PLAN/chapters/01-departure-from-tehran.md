# Chapter 1: Departure from Tehran
**Route:** Tehran → Qazvin → Zanjan → Taserkand → Mianeh → Tabriz
**Dates:** Dec 19–20, 1939
**Mode of travel:** Hired car with chauffeur (Fritz, Strauss, an Armenian girl and a young man traveling with her)

> Citation format: `[Letter p. N — "anchor"]` points to the sentence in letter scan N whose English contains the anchor phrase. Each one resolves to a sentence ID in `PLAN/letter/pages/page-NN.json` (see design doc, "Primary Source & Translation Engine").

## Chapter Overview
Fritz leaves his parents and almost three years of refuge in Tehran. In two days a hired car carries the party 600 km across the snowy Iranian plateau to Tabriz, where they find the luggage bus they had sent ahead and arrange the next stage toward the Turkish border.

---

## Stop: Tehran
**Coordinates:** 35.6892° N, 51.3890° E | **Date:** Dec 19, 1939, 5:00 PM

### Narrative
The goodbye came suddenly. Everything Fritz had wanted to say "remained stuck in my chest" [Letter p. 1 — "remained stuck in my chest"](#p01-s06). He waited almost 2 hours at the garage hoping his parents would come by once more, grateful for two years of friendships and, not least, for having got his parents out of "the immediate danger zone" [Letter p. 1 — "the immediate danger zone"](#p01-s11). At a quarter to five the fourth traveler, an Armenian girl, arrived with an enormous amount of luggage and a crowd of relatives. After a noisy repacking they drove off at 5 o'clock, Fritz squeezed in the middle of the back seat, leaving Tehran in the twilight with hardly a word spoken [Letter p. 1 — "Finally, at 5 o'clock we drove off"](#p01-s15).

### Features & Media
* **Context Card:** Tehran in 1939: Reza Shah's modernizing capital and a refuge for Central European Jewish emigrants (Fritz and Strauss were Jewish dentists bound for America).
* **Primary Source Highlight:** The farewell paragraph [Letter p. 1 — "Our farewell today 6 days ago"](#p01-s06).

## Stop: Qazvin
**Coordinates:** 36.2688° N, 49.9981° E | **Date:** Dec 19, 1939, 7:30 PM

### Narrative
They stopped at the "Grand Hotel" for a short rest. Strauss, who had spent four days there on his way to Tehran, rushed to the hotel's venerable radio set "without noteworthy success" [Letter p. 1 — "venerable radio set"](#p01-s22). Fritz ordered borscht and tea. Because they looked affluent, they were charged 8 Kran for the meager snack [Letter p. 1 — "they took 8 Kran from us"](#p01-s25).

### Features & Media
* **Archival Photography:** Qazvin in the 1930s (see `PLAN/media/chapter-01.md`).
* **Cultural Card:** Iranian tea culture: the samovar, glass tea cups and roadside teahouses (*chaikhaneh*).

## Leg: Qazvin → Zanjan (night, snowfall)
### Narrative
They drove on through the snowfall and reached Zanjan around midnight [Letter p. 1 — "where we arrived around midnight"](#p01-s26).

## Stop: Zanjan
**Coordinates:** 36.6736° N, 48.4963° E | **Date:** Dec 19–20, 1939 (midnight to 8:30 AM)

### Narrative
The town's only hotel had most rooms locked and the keys lost. After the girl and the young man had rooms, a door was forced for Fritz and Strauss, revealing one bed. It was bitterly cold, so they shared it [Letter p. 1 — "and we lay down"](#p01-s30). The young man then burst in to complain that his window had no panes. In the morning Fritz worked out that shutters, not glass, were the local custom [Letter p. 2 — "came in again and swore"](#p02-s01).

### Features & Media
* **Journal Highlight:** The "missing windowpanes" episode, a light comic beat before the hardships ahead.

## Leg: Zanjan → Taserkand (morning)
### Narrative
They left at 8:30–9:00. The hilly landscape was unexpectedly free of snow. The Armenian girl was carsick the whole way, and then it began to snow again. Fritz "had reason once again to thank my fate that I sat in the back" [Letter p. 2 — "fate that I sat in the back"](#p02-s07).

## Leg: Mianeh → Tabriz (afternoon)
### Narrative
Fritz and Strauss suddenly stepped into the road at the same moment. In front of a lone teahouse, exactly 100 km before Tabriz, stood the bus carrying their luggage, which had already been on the road for four days [Letter p. 2 — "exactly 100 km before Tabriz"](#p02-s11). The road then climbed in endless serpentines over a cloud-wrapped mountain, where the chauffeur pointed out the place where two Turkish airplanes had crashed after the Crown Prince's wedding [Letter p. 2 — "2 Turkish airplanes flew into the mountain"](#p02-s13).

### Features & Media
* **Historical Spotter:** The Turkish aircraft lost after Crown Prince Mohammad Reza's March 1939 wedding to Princess Fawzia (verify details; the letter mentions the wreckage again at Bazargan, p. 4).
* **Animated Route Segment:** Serpentine road animation into the clouds.

## Stop: Tabriz
**Coordinates:** 38.0962° N, 46.2738° E | **Date:** Dec 20, 1939, 6:30 PM (overnight)

### Narrative
Over tea, Hamid-Aga, a garage owner recommended by Mr. Fischer, said a truck would leave for Trebizond (Trabzon) on Thursday and would try to take them [Letter p. 2 — "Hamid-Aga, the garage owner"](#p02-s14). They took what was supposedly the last room at the Grand Hotel, went to the cinema (and slept there), and slept again at the hotel [Letter p. 2 — "decided to go to the cinema"](#p02-s16).

### Features & Media
* **Archival Photography:** 1930s Tabriz, the bazaar and the Grand Hotel (see `PLAN/media/chapter-01.md`).
