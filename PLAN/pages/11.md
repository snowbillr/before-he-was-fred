# Page 11: City — Arrival & Logistics in Tabriz
**Coordinates:** 38.0962° N, 46.2738° E | **Date:** Dec 24–25, 1939

### Left Column: Narrative
Arriving in Tabriz at 5:30 PM, they met Hamid-Aga, a garage owner recommended by Mr. Fischer, who began arranging forward transport on a 1.5-ton Chevrolet truck bound for Trabzon[cite: 1, 2, 3]. After securing the last available room at the local Grand Hotel and attending a movie at the cinema, Fritz changed his pinching shoes at the garage the next morning[cite: 1, 1]. A local Lithuanian-Jewish pharmacist translated via French, aiding them in securing bus tickets to Bazargan and forwarding their heavy luggage[cite: 1, 3].

### Right Column: Features & Media
* **Cultural Feature Card:** *Sangak & Lavash Flatbread ("Hand-Towels")* — Examination of stone-baked flatbreads wrapped in cloth (*sofreh*) and rolling *Luqmeh* (bites of cheese and fat) for long journeys[cite: 2, 3].
* **Archival Photography:** Historic views of 1930s Tabriz, the bazaar, and local transport garages[cite: 1, 2].
