# Page 26: Transit — Greece & Italy to Naples
**Coordinates & Route:** Pythion → Salonika → Piraeus → Corinth Canal → Brindisi → Naples | **Date:** Jan 19–24, 1940

### Left Column: Narrative
Departing via the Orient Express on January 19, they crossed Greece through Pythion, Alexandroupolis, and Thessaloniki to Piraeus[cite: 1, 2, 3]. On January 21, they sprinted onto the steamer *SS Egitto*, which was towed through the narrow Corinth Canal[cite: 1, 2, 3, 4]. Landing at Brindisi on January 23, Fritz missed his train due to baggage delays and took an overnight bus through Foggia to reunite with Strauss in Naples[cite: 1, 3].
