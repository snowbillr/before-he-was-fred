# Page 24: City — Erzurum & Erzincan to Sivas
**Coordinates & Route:** Erzurum → Erzincan → Sivas | **Date:** Jan 8–14, 1940

### Left Column: Narrative
Arriving in Erzurum on January 8, they found train service disrupted by severe weather and the catastrophic Erzincan earthquake[cite: 1, 3]. Short on funds, Strauss sold dental impression trays as cookie molds to locals[cite: 2, 3]. Boarding a train on January 11, they passed through earthquake ruins at Erzincan, crossed the Çetinkaya junction, and waited overnight at the Sivas police station in -28°C weather[cite: 1, 3].

### Right Column: Features & Media
* **Disaster History Card:** The 1939 Erzincan Earthquake damage along the Anatolian railway[cite: 1, 2, 3].
* **Artifact Callout:** The dental impression trays (*Abdrucklöffel*) sold for funds[cite: 2, 3].
