# Page 10: Transit — Mianeh to Tabriz
**Coordinates & Route:** Mianeh → Tabriz | **Date:** Dec 24, 1939 (Afternoon)

### Left Column: Narrative
Exactly 100 kilometers before Tabriz, Fritz and Strauss were relieved to spot their luggage bus stranded outside a teahouse, where it had been delayed for four days[cite: 1, 3]. Continuing over winding mountain serpentines blanketed in clouds, their chauffeur pointed out the crash site where two Turkish aircraft had previously flown into the mountain following the Crown Prince's wedding[cite: 1].
