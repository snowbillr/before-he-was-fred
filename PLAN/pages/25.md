# Page 25: City — Ankara, Eskişehir & Istanbul, Turkey
**Coordinates & Route:** Ankara → Eskişehir → Istanbul | **Date:** Jan 15–18, 1940

### Left Column: Narrative
Reaching Ankara on January 15 for their first warm meal in four days, they continued past Eskişehir to Istanbul’s Haydarpaşa terminal on January 16[cite: 1, 3]. Crossing by ferry to Pera, Fritz received medical treatment at a university clinic for a severe foot abscess[cite: 1, 3]. Assisted by local contacts, they resolved police registration issues and secured passage on an Italian steamer[cite: 1, 3].
