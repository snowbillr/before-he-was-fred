# Page 1: City — Departure from Tehran
**Coordinates:** 35.6892° N, 51.3890° E | **Date:** Dec 23, 1939 (Evening)

### Left Column: Narrative
On the evening of December 23, 1939, Fritz and Strauss prepared to leave Tehran[cite: 1, 2]. After a long delay waiting for their fourth travel companion—an Armenian girl who arrived around 4:30–5:00 PM with immense amounts of luggage and provision baskets—they loaded their hired car[cite: 1]. Accompanied by a young man traveling with the girl, the three men crammed into the vehicle with Fritz sitting in the middle of the back seat[cite: 1]. Driven by an excellent chauffeur, they departed into the twilight[cite: 1].
