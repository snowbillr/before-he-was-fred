# Page 22: City — Türkali & Tahir Pass, Turkey
**Coordinates:** ~39.8300° N, 42.4500° E | **Date:** Jan 1–6, 1940

### Left Column: Narrative
Attempting the high Tahir Pass on New Year's Day, the group was trapped in a blinding blizzard[cite: 1, 2, 3]. Strauss sank neck-deep into snow drifts, and a horse fell down a ravine[cite: 2, 3]. Gunshots were fired into the storm for help[cite: 2, 3]. Forced to retreat, they were stranded in a vermin-infested shelter in Türkali until January 6, when horse sleds cleared the pass[cite: 1, 3].
