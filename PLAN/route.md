# Chronological Travel Route

Below is the step-by-step route of the journey from Tehran, Iran to Hasankale, Turkey (December 1939 – January 1940):

## Tehran, Iran 35.6892° N, 51.3890° E

Departure: Dec 23, 1939 (evening) via a hired car.

## Qazvin, Iran 36.2688° N, 49.9981° E

Arrival: Dec 23, 1939 (8:30 PM). Stopped for a quick rest at the Grand Hotel.

## Zanjan, Iran 36.6736° N, 48.4963° E

Arrival: Dec 23/24, 1939 (Midnight). Overnight stay.

## Taserkand (Tazeh Kand), Iran ~37.3000° N, 47.7000° E

Arrival: Dec 24, 1939 (10:00 AM). Brief tea break.

## Mianeh, Iran 37.4173° N, 47.7121° E

Arrival: Dec 24, 1939 (Noon). Lunch stop.

## Tabriz, Iran 38.0962° N, 46.2738° E

Arrival: Dec 24, 1939 (5:30 PM). Stayed at the Grand Hotel; arranged transport via Chevrolet truck.

## Khoi (Khoy), Iran 38.5524° N, 44.9521° E

Arrival: Dec 25/26, 1939 (1:00 AM). Overnight stay.

## Maku, Iran 39.2931° N, 44.4533° E

Arrival: Dec 26, 1939 (11:00 PM). Overnight stay in a bus waiting room.

## Bazirgan (Bazargan), Iran 39.3891° N, 44.3855° E

Arrival: Dec 27, 1939 (Morning). Iranian border post near Mount Ararat; sent a telegram home.

## Gürbulak, Turkey 39.3950° N, 44.3800° E

Arrival: Dec 27, 1939. Border crossing point into Turkey; processed customs.

## Bayazit (Doğubayazıt), Turkey 39.5461° N, 44.0839° E

Arrival: Dec 27, 1939 (Evening). Dinner stop.

## Kizildice (Kızıldiz / Kızıldizce), Turkey ~39.6300° N, 43.8300° E

Arrival: Dec 27, 1939 (Night). Delayed due to snow; changed vehicles to a Ford truck.

## Taşlıçay, Turkey 39.6328° N, 43.3556° E

Arrival: Dec 28, 1939 (Early morning). Car broke down; stranded for multiple days waiting for fuel.

## Karaköse (Ağrı), Turkey 39.7194° N, 43.0503° E

Arrival: Dec 29, 1939 (Evening). Arrived via sled; stayed until Dec 31 due to road closures.

## Eleşkirt (Alaskirt), Turkey 39.7944° N, 42.6739° E

Arrival: Dec 31, 1939 (4:00 PM). Spent New Year's Eve here.

## Türkali (Tahir Pass vicinity), Turkey ~39.8300° N, 42.4500° E

Arrival: Jan 1, 1940 (10:00 AM). Attempted Tahir Pass, got caught in a severe blizzard, forced to turn back. Stranded here until Jan 6.

## Delal, Turkey ~39.8500° N, 42.2500° E

Arrival: Jan 6, 1940 (8:30 PM). Successfully crossed the Tahir Pass via horse-drawn sleds.

## Horasan (Horassan), Turkey 40.0450° N, 42.1733° E

Arrival: Jan 7, 1940 (Morning). Reached the railway line.

## Hasankale (Pasinler), Turkey 39.9806° N, 41.6708° E

Arrival: Jan 7, 1940. Final location recorded in this portion of the chronicle.

## Erzurum, Turkey 39.9043° N, 41.2679° E

Arrival: Jan 8, 1940 (Morning).

Details: Arrived via horse sled. Stayed until Jan 11 due to railway delays caused by severe weather and earthquake damage. Boarded the train on Jan 11 at 9:00 AM.

## Erzincan, Turkey 39.7500° N, 39.5000° E

Arrival: Jan 12, 1940.

Details: Epicenter of the recent massive earthquake; stopped for 4 hours amidst severe destruction and transferred trains.

## Çetinkaya (Kangal), Turkey 39.2500° N, 37.6167° E

Arrival: Jan 13, 1940 (5:00 PM).

Details: Railway junction point where the southern line splits off; purchased provisions during a brief stop.

## Sivas, Turkey 39.7477° N, 37.0179° E

Arrival: Jan 13, 1940 (9:00 PM).

Details: Waited overnight at the police station due to train delays in extreme cold (-28°C) before continuing westward.

## Ankara, Turkey 39.9334° N, 32.8597° E

Arrival: Jan 15, 1940 (Noon).

Details: Capital city stopover; ate their first warm meal in 4 days at the railway station.

## Eskişehir, Turkey 39.7767° N, 30.5206° E

Arrival: Jan 15, 1940 (Evening).

Details: 2-hour layover to get tea and bread.

## Istanbul (Haydarpaşa & Pera), Turkey 41.0082° N, 28.9784° E

Arrival: Jan 16, 1940 (7:00 AM).

Details: Arrived at Haydarpaşa terminal on the Asian side, took the ferry across to Pera (European side). Delayed by police processing, fines, and emergency medical treatment for a foot abscess.

## Pythion, Greece 41.3783° N, 26.6119° E

Arrival: Jan 19, 1940 (Morning).

Details: Crossed the border via the Orient Express. Missed the connecting train to Athens due to delays.

## Alexandroupolis, Greece 40.8457° N, 25.8739° E

Arrival: Jan 20, 1940.

Details: Transferred trains after traveling across Thrace in a diesel railcar.

## Salonika (Thessaloniki), Greece 40.6401° N, 22.9444° E

Arrival: Jan 20, 1940 (10:00 PM).

Details: Overnight train stopover en route to Athens.

## Piraeus / Athens, Greece 37.9475° N, 23.6372° E

Arrival: Jan 21, 1940 (11:30 AM).

Details: Passed through Thebes, arrived at Piraeus port, and boarded the steamer Egitto bound for Italy at noon.

## Isthmus of Corinth, Greece 37.9264° N, 22.9886° E

Arrival: Jan 21, 1940 (Late afternoon).

Details: Maritime transit; towed by a tugboat through the narrow Corinth Canal into the Ionian Sea.

## Brindisi, Italy 40.6327° N, 17.9419° E

Arrival: Jan 23, 1940 (11:00 AM).

Details: Port of entry into Italy. Fritz missed the direct train due to luggage delays and took a 3:00 PM bus through Foggia to reach Naples overnight.

## Naples (Napoli), Italy 40.8518° N, 14.2681° E

Arrival: Jan 24, 1940 (6:30 AM).

Details: Reunited with travel companions at the port and boarded the transatlantic ocean liner Conte di Savoia at noon.

## Gibraltar 36.1408° N, 5.3536° W

Arrival: Jan 26, 1940.

Details: Anchored for 6 hours while British authorities inspected the vessel before entering the Atlantic Ocean.

## New York City (Brooklyn), USA 40.6782° N, 73.9442° W

Arrival: Early February 1940 (Letter written on Feb 21, 1940).

Details: Final destination; arrived via transatlantic crossing across the Atlantic Ocean.
