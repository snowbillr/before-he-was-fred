# Chronological Travel Route

Below is the step-by-step route of the whole journey from Tehran, Iran to New York City (December 19, 1939 – about January 31, 1940). Each entry lists the stop, then (in *italics*) how they traveled to the next stop. The chapter files in `chapters/` carry the narrative and letter citations for both stops and legs.

> **Dates:** The letter's German dateline reads "25. Dezember 1939, Taslicay" (the English translation had "29"), and Fritz writes that the farewell in Tehran was "heute vor 6 Tagen" (6 days ago). So they left Tehran on Dec 19, reached Bazargan on Dec 23 (matching the telegram "vom 23."), and woke in Taşlıçay on Dec 25. The Dec 25 and Dec 27 dates on letter pp. 6–7 are correct.

## Tehran, Iran 35.6892° N, 51.3890° E

Departure: Dec 19, 1939, 5:00 PM (twilight), in a hired car with chauffeur.

*Leg: hired car through light snowfall.*

## Qazvin, Iran 36.2688° N, 49.9981° E

Arrival: Dec 19, 1939 (7:30 PM). Stopped for a quick rest at the Grand Hotel.

## Zanjan, Iran 36.6736° N, 48.4963° E

Arrival: Dec 19/20, 1939 (Midnight). Overnight stay.

## Taserkand (Tazeh Kand), Iran ~37.3000° N, 47.7000° E

Arrival: Dec 20, 1939 (10:00 AM). Brief tea break.

## Mianeh, Iran 37.4173° N, 47.7121° E

Arrival: Dec 20, 1939 (Noon). Lunch stop.

## Tabriz, Iran 38.0962° N, 46.2738° E

Arrival: Dec 20, 1939 (6:30 PM). Stayed at the Grand Hotel. On Dec 21, with help from the pharmacist Isaac Rabinowitsch, booked a 1.5-ton Chevrolet truck to Trabzon (130 Toman). The truck carried the luggage; they took the bus.

*Leg: overcrowded night bus along the Tabriz–Julfa railway, departing ~5:15 PM Dec 21.*

## Khoi (Khoy), Iran 38.5524° N, 44.9521° E

Arrival: Dec 22, 1939 (1:00 AM). Overnight stay; bought wool socks in the bazaar.

*Leg: ancient junk-laden bus departing 3:00 PM, stopping every 2 km to refill the radiator; tea in an opium den at dusk.*

## Maku, Iran 39.2931° N, 44.4533° E

Arrival: Dec 22, 1939 (11:00 PM). Slept on two benches in the bus waiting room.

*Leg: first ride in the three-seat Chevrolet, departing 9:00 AM Dec 23.*

## Bazirgan (Bazargan), Iran 39.3891° N, 44.3855° E

Arrival: Dec 23, 1939 (Morning). Iranian customs and police. The two dentists advised the customs director on his two bad teeth. Ararat was hidden in fog. Sent a telegram home and ate lunch in Abdil's mud hut.

## Gürbulak, Turkey 39.3950° N, 44.3800° E

Arrival: Dec 23, 1939 (afternoon). Turkish customs, about 4 km from Bazargan. Two new tires were confiscated from the driver.

## Bayazit (Doğubayazıt), Turkey 39.5461° N, 44.0839° E

Arrival: Dec 23, 1939 (after dark). Dinner chosen by pointing at the pots in the kitchen.

*Leg: Chevrolet, 25 km through thickening snow.*

## Kizildice (Kızıldiz / Kızıldizce), Turkey ~39.6300° N, 43.8300° E

Arrival: Dec 23, 1939 (Night). Snowbound all day Dec 24 in a room with ten cots, playing 66. Transferred to a windowless Ford truck, with a Chevrolet carrying Strauss's large luggage.

*Leg: night drive in deep snow, shoveling and pushing; the Chevrolet ran out of gasoline and they sheltered in a lonely house.*

## Taşlıçay, Turkey 39.6328° N, 43.3556° E

Arrival: Dec 25, 1939 (pre-dawn). −35° overnight. Waited about two days for gasoline from Karaköse; Fritz began the letter here (dateline 25 Dec).

*Leg: the Ford broke an axle leaving town; rented a horse sled; passed the overturned Chevrolet.*

## Karaköse (Ağrı), Turkey 39.7194° N, 43.0503° E

Arrival: Dec 27, 1939 (at dark). Arrived by sled. The road was closed to cars, so they stayed until the morning of Dec 31 and then hired sleds toward Erzurum (185 km).

*Leg: horse sled, 35 km.*

## Eleşkirt (Alaskirt), Turkey 39.7944° N, 42.6739° E

Arrival: Dec 31, 1939 (4:00 PM, already dark). New Year's Eve with tea and roast mutton.

*Leg: three sleds, 22 km uphill in drizzle, departing at dawn on Jan 1.*

## Türkali (Tahir Pass vicinity), Turkey ~39.8300° N, 42.4500° E

Arrival: Jan 1, 1940 (10:00 AM). Attempted the Tahir Pass (toward Tahir village, 18 km on), was caught in a blizzard just below the ridge, and turned back after more than 2 hours. Stranded in a hut and then with the gendarmes until Jan 6.

*Leg: Jan 6, 6:00 AM, walking behind the luggage sleds over the pass to Tahir (10:00 AM), then 20 km downhill by sled.*

## Delal, Turkey ~39.8500° N, 42.2500° E

Arrival: Jan 6, 1940 (8:30 PM). Four houses and an inn, past the mountains.

*Leg: caravan of four sleds, departing 6:00 AM Jan 7.*

## Horasan (Horassan), Turkey 40.0450° N, 42.1733° E

Arrival: Jan 7, 1940 (Morning). Reached the railway line, but the next train to Erzurum was 5 days away, so they kept going by sled into a headwind.

## Hasankale (Pasinler), Turkey 39.9806° N, 41.6708° E

Arrival: Jan 7, 1940 (6:00 PM, after 11½ hours of travel). First warm room and clean beds since Tabriz.

*Leg: sled, 35 km over a mountain road between 3 m snow walls, Jan 8.*

## Erzurum, Turkey 39.9043° N, 41.2679° E

Arrival: Jan 8, 1940 (Morning).

Details: Arrived via horse sled. Stayed until Jan 11 due to railway delays caused by severe weather and earthquake damage. Boarded the train on Jan 11; it pulled out at 9:00 AM (Letter p. 13). The 12.1.40, 13:15 entry says they had been on it for about 30 hours.

*Leg: third-class train, repeatedly stuck in snow; a night without food or water; 30 hours to the landslide about 150 km out (Jan 12, 13:15); transfer on foot 200 m to a second train; 50 km in 4 hours to Erzincan.*

*Landslide waypoint (for the globe): on the Erzurum–Erzincan railway, about 50 km east of Erzincan by rail. The letter gives no place name, so plot it by distance along the line and label it approximate.*

## Erzincan, Turkey 39.7500° N, 39.5000° E

Arrival: Jan 12, 1940 (evening).

Details: Epicenter of the 27 Dec 1939 earthquake. A 4-hour stop; they walked through the ruins (the letter estimates 13,000 dead of 18,000 inhabitants). Overnight on the train, playing poker and 66 with officers.

## Çetinkaya (Kangal), Turkey 39.2500° N, 37.6167° E

Arrival: Jan 13, 1940 (5:00 PM).

Details: Railway junction point where the southern line splits off; purchased provisions during a brief stop.

## Sivas, Turkey 39.7477° N, 37.0179° E

Arrival: Jan 13, 1940 (9:00 PM).

Details: Waited overnight at the police station due to train delays in extreme cold (-28°C) before continuing westward.

## Ankara, Turkey 39.9334° N, 32.8597° E

Arrival: Jan 15, 1940 (Noon).

Details: Capital city stopover; ate their first warm meal in 4 days at the railway station.

## Eskişehir, Turkey 39.7767° N, 30.5206° E

Arrival: Jan 15, 1940 (Evening; the 11 PM entry was written after leaving).

Details: 2-hour layover to get tea and bread.

## Istanbul (Haydarpaşa & Galata), Turkey 40.9969° N, 29.0192° E (Haydarpaşa terminal)

Ferry landing: Karaköy (Galata), 41.0225° N, 28.9744° E. The `Haydarpaşa → Galata` ferry leg runs between these two points.

Arrival: Jan 16, 1940 (7:00 AM).

Details: Arrived at Haydarpaşa terminal on the Asian side, took the ferry across to Galata (European side; the typescript says "Pera", corrected by hand to "Galata"). Delayed by police processing, fines, and emergency medical treatment for a foot abscess.

## Pythion, Greece 41.3783° N, 26.6119° E

Arrival: Jan 20, 1940 (Morning).

Details: Left Istanbul Jan 19 at 10:30 PM on the Orient Express in a compartment with a police escort, under a same-day deportation order. The express was late and the Athens connection had gone; they waited 24 hours and slept in the waiting room.

## Alexandroupolis, Greece 40.8457° N, 25.8739° E

Arrival: Jan 21, 1940.

Details: Transferred trains after a diesel railcar across Thrace (flooded river plain, cranes).

## Salonika (Thessaloniki), Greece 40.6401° N, 22.9444° E

Arrival: Jan 21, 1940 (10:00 PM).

Details: Overnight train stopover en route to Athens.

## Piraeus / Athens, Greece 37.9475° N, 23.6372° E

Arrival: Jan 22, 1940 (11:30 AM).

Details: Passed Thebes, went straight to Piraeus, and boarded the 3,500-ton steamer *Egitto* for Brindisi at noon, at the last second. Letter pages 18–19 (covering part of this stretch) are missing.

## Isthmus of Corinth, Greece 37.9264° N, 22.9886° E

Arrival: Jan 22, 1940 (Late afternoon).

Details: Maritime transit; towed by a tugboat through the narrow Corinth Canal into the Ionian Sea.

## Brindisi, Italy 40.6327° N, 17.9419° E

Arrival: Jan 23, 1940 (11:00 AM).

Details: Port of entry into Italy. Fritz missed the 1:00 PM train to Naples because of baggage delays (Strauss caught it). He took a 3:00 PM train to Foggia and another train after midnight.

## Naples (Napoli), Italy 40.8518° N, 14.2681° E

Arrival: Jan 24, 1940 (6:30 AM).

Details: Met Strauss, who was already waiting, and watched the *Conte di Savoia* arrive at 9:00. It sailed exactly at noon.

## Gibraltar 36.1408° N, 5.3536° W

Arrival: Jan 26, 1940.

Details: Anchored for 6 hours while British authorities inspected the vessel before entering the Atlantic Ocean.

## New York City, USA 40.6782° N, 73.9442° W

Arrival: ~Jan 31, 1940 (the Feb 21 Brooklyn section says they arrived "3 weeks ago"; consistent with a Jan 24 departure; confirm via passenger manifest). The final section of the letter is dated Brooklyn, Feb 21, 1940. Italian Line ships docked at the Manhattan West Side piers; the coordinates mark Brooklyn, where the letter was finished.

Details: Seasick for most of the Atlantic crossing. Manhattan's skyscrapers rose out of the haze as they arrived. The letter does not mention the Statue of Liberty.
